Why Caregivers Need Love Too

It's More Than You Think!

We talk a lot about supporting patients, but what about the people holding everything together behind the scenes? Caregivers and family members often pour every bit of their energy into helping their loved ones—while their own well-being quietly takes a backseat.

It's time we shine a light on them.

Here's the reality:

😰

88%

of caregivers experience significant stress

Nearly half feel emotionally exhausted - Cleveland Clinic

And it doesn't stop there. According to the CDC, long-term caregivers face higher risks of chronic illnesses like heart disease and depression. They're giving so much that their own health is often compromised.

Let's not forget the financial strain. The Alzheimer's Association shared that dementia caregivers spend around $7,000 to $12,000 of their own money each year.

That's like buying a used car—every year!

But there's hope. When caregivers receive support—whether through respite care, emotional check-ins, or financial help—they become stronger, healthier, and better equipped to care. A little help goes a long way.

So let's do this:

Reach out to a caregiver today. Offer a hug, a meal, or just your time.
Because supporting those who support others isn't just kind—it's necessary.
